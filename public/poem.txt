the wet, market smile of her forgotten face, still
spreading our neighbourly chill around the ward
all together chinked by the overhearing
of a lightweight’s glassy, laboured breathing
today's creamiest wight, a newly lost Israelite
never quite there in their universalism
the superstitious cleaving to silence's rift
stuttered into being before his every, last word
too openly expectant in bright yellow
for a darling in the palm of a meaty hand
honestly, never small enough to
sink into the nothing special
fluttering through the sky's empty promise
face first into its shards of brilliance
on the verge of ghosting her full on
sheer unbelievability, as far as that goes
the man waving to my woman as she rides
me, like a mare down May's flowery avenues
holding out for more sex appeal
than anyone could deal with
too good to let his fragile shelter inside these
years of contempt that press on, homeless
caught in the overflow of a mirror room,
on the inland echo of her empty womb
the youthful rebellion playing
right into a blazing pyre
of the stupid billions, one expression
finally saying what they’d really been thinking
a blind wail unable to cut through
the thinning innocence of their blues
this narrow, circular talk most of all outlasting
the vicious reality of everyone and everything else
yesterday's balloon long gone fishing for dad:
the white knight on a quest to name his cover band
original as every gangster lashing out at mother:
bitch never really liked me, she only ever loved me
an ancient bend dimly lit down Indian road
following the cheerful path of least resistance
old news from the front lines
blowing up any second – now
almost pulling off the swagger
of it can only get better
touching myself right there 
in the open: something of God’s
tearing past the humility in –
between shame and pride
turning over envy only to find them
seething through me, so jealously
interrupting the dead beast's dream
of once and for all scrubbing off clean
the faith of baby’s outstretched arm
waiting on whatever's taking so long
kicking and screaming at the unflinching
politeness of embarrassment’s hush
stomaching the air above
a childishly direct question
unprepared for them, so close
up the closer we find ourselves
another round on the house without
reason, short of that family feeling
in love with the sound of a name, repeated
under his very own breath again – again
purely overcome by the flooding, nauseous
flush of misfortune's bottomless rush
whistling past the new girl next door
having already seen it, seen it all before
calling out every stranger I can
lined up with a bat in hand
eclipsing the moon with one unaddressed,
worldly look: dressing down his poetry.
